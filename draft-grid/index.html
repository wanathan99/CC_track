Draft Grid

A mystery NBA team's first pick from each of the last 16 drafts. Name the picks. Name the team.

How to play

  • A random NBA team is chosen and kept secret.
  • The grid shows one cell per draft year, 2026 down to 2011, each with the logo of the school/club of that team's first pick in that draft — usually round 1, but round 2 when that's all they had (tagged 2nd rd). Gray cells mean that team made no pick at all that year.
  • Guess each pick by name, and guess the mystery team, using the shared pool of guesses below. Guess in any order.
  • Wrong guesses cost a life, correct guesses are free. Run out of lives before finishing and the round is a loss — everything still hidden gets revealed.
  • You need every open cell and the team itself correct to win — getting all 16 picks right but never landing the team still ends in a loss if you run out of lives.

Good to know

  • "First pick" means whoever the team actually had on the clock that year — if that player was immediately traded away on draft night, the pick still counts for the original team, not wherever the player ended up.
  • Team identity follows the current franchise, not whatever it was called that year: Charlotte Bobcats picks count for today's Hornets, New Jersey Nets picks count for today's Brooklyn Nets, and the 2011–2012 New Orleans Hornets count for today's Pelicans (not the current Charlotte Hornets, which is a different lineage).
  • Name guesses accept the full name or just the last name, and ignore accents, punctuation, and capitalization.
  • A team can go multiple years without any pick at all — that's a real gray streak, not a bug.
  • A handful of obscure international/prep-school picks show a colored initials badge instead of a logo where no reliable crest was available.
